✦ Handle with care

Hard Sayings of Jesus

Difficult words explained simply and carefully

“Hate father and mother”

Not cruelty — priority. Love for Jesus must come first; every other love is second (see Matthew 10:37).

Luke 14:26

“If your eye causes you to sin, tear it out”

Strong picture language: deal radically with sin. Jesus is not commanding self-harm; He is commanding serious holiness.

Matthew 5:29–30

“I did not come to bring peace, but a sword”

The gospel brings true peace with God, yet it can divide households when some believe and others refuse.

Matthew 10:34

“Eat my flesh and drink my blood”

Jesus speaks of living by faith in His sacrificed life — not cannibalism. John 6 points to believing and remaining in Him.

John 6:53–56

“Let the dead bury their own dead”

The call of the kingdom is urgent. Even good family duties must not delay full obedience to Jesus.

Luke 9:60

“Be perfect, as your heavenly Father is perfect”

The standard of kingdom love is God’s own character — driving us to grace, not proud self-salvation.

Matthew 5:48

How to Use This Hub

How to use this hub

  1. Start with a free book, or open the Bible reader.
  2. Save verses that speak to you — they stay on your device.
  3. Use Resources for the reading plan, tools, and flyer.

What we do · What we do not

  • We do offer free Christian books, Scripture tools, and prayer helps.
  • We do keep chats and saved verses on your device when possible.
  • We do point people to Jesus Christ and the written Word.
  • We do not sell your data or charge for the books on this hub.
  • We do not replace your local church, pastor, or personal Bible study.
  • We do not claim the AI is infallible — test everything by Scripture.
